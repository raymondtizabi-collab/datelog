Private fields — nickname, notes, where, and any “other” details — are never shared. “Other” answers count only as “other”. Ratings and checkbox categories may be counted.

Exactly what gets shared?

Only when the toggle is on, each reviewed (non-demo) date is sent once as one anonymous row with these fields — nothing else:

  • When: month only (e.g. 2026-10) and date number with that person (1st, 2nd…)
  • Date: date type (coffee/drinks/dinner/activity/other) · how you met (app name or in person/work/set up/other) · online vs in person
  • Ratings (1–5): excitement before · how it went · your rating of them · category ratings (looks, personality, conversation, humor, chemistry, values)
  • Outcome: would you see them again · did they want to · 2nd date yes/no/not yet
  • Money: spend bucket (e.g. $26–50, never the amount) · who paid · who you expected to pay
  • Fixed-choice tags: what drew you in · hoping for · concerns · why you passed · why they passed (preset choices only; “Other” is sent as just “Other”)
  • You: age band, gender (blank unless you set them) · city (Los Angeles)
  • Separately, we count anonymous usage (e.g. a date was logged), never what’s in it: a random id and the event name only.

Never leaves your device: nicknames, private notes, places/venues, profile screenshots, any typed “other” text, exact dates or times, your name, friends’ names or predictions.

Share my anonymous counts (off by default)
