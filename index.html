Peer numbers are shown only for groups with at least 5 dates. Turn this off anytime to stop sharing and re-lock. When on, each reviewed date is sent once as an anonymous row: answer choices, 1–5 numbers, spend bucket and month only — no names, notes, places, text or exact dates. Turning it off stops new uploads; rows already sent can’t be linked back to you and aren’t deleted. Your personal stats are always free.
